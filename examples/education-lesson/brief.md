# Brief — Education lesson example

- **Topic:** Introducing recursion to first-year CS students.
- **Mode:** education
- **Audience:** novices who can already write variables and functions.
- **Goal:** learners can trace a simple recursive function and identify its base case.
- **Time budget:** ~12 minutes (~12 slides).
- **Style:** soft-clay-3d
- **Objectives (Bloom's):**
  - understand — explain what a base case is and why it's required
  - apply — trace a recursive call by hand to its result
- **Concepts + prerequisites:** functions → base-case → recursive-case → recursion (full).
