# Japanese Editorial — Washi & Soft Glow  (`japanese-editorial`)

Calm and editorial, warm paper tones, generous whitespace. Reflective and design-literate.

- **Use for:** narrative/reflective decks, design audiences, brand storytelling.
- **Palette:** warm paper bg, soft charcoal ink, muted red + pine-green accents.
- **Feel:** serif headings, `・` bullets, no heavy rules, a quiet circle motif.
- **Avoid for:** aggressive sales decks or dense dashboards.

Excels at `quote` and single-idea `content` slides with one supporting image.
Tokens: `assets/style-tokens/japanese-editorial.json`.
