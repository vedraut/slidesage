# Japanese Hand-Drawn Editorial  (`hand-drawn-editorial`)

Organic and sketched. Feels human and low-pressure — like a great whiteboard session.

- **Use for:** workshops, ideation sessions, informal or exploratory teaching.
- **Palette:** off-white paper bg, warm charcoal ink, terracotta + sage accents.
- **Feel:** rounded/casual heading face, en-dash bullets, no rules, soft motifs.
- **Avoid for:** formal executive or investor settings.

Pairs well with `worked-example`, `agenda`, and open-ended `retrieval` prompts.
Tokens: `assets/style-tokens/hand-drawn-editorial.json`.
