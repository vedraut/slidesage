# Corporate Bright  (`corporate-bright`)

A clean, light alternative to `futuristic-tech` for audiences who want a bright deck. White canvas, deep-navy
ink, blue + green corporate accents — pairs well with a brand logo.

- **Use for:** technical business demos, internal reviews, brand-aligned decks, anything that should feel
  bright and modern rather than dark.
- **Palette:** white bg, navy ink, blue accent, green secondary; chart palette adds red/amber/violet.
- **Feel:** sans-serif, accent rule under titles, blue block on the cover, section bands.
- **Avoid for:** moody/cinematic reveals (use `futuristic-tech`) or austere luxury (use `minimalist-luxury`).

Pairs naturally with the `--logo` option (top-right brand mark). If your logo has a light/white wordmark,
recolor it for light backgrounds first. Tokens: `assets/style-tokens/corporate-bright.json`.
