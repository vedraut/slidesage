# Futuristic Tech Editorial  (`futuristic-tech`)

**Business default.** Dark, high-contrast, data-driven. Conveys confidence and precision.

- **Use for:** product launches, strategy, fintech, metrics-heavy investor decks.
- **Palette:** near-black bg, off-white ink, teal accent — numbers and charts pop.
- **Feel:** sharp sans-serif, thin accent rule under titles, diagonal accent bands on the cover.
- **Avoid for:** soft/warm topics, children's education, hand-crafted brands.

Pairs well with hero stats and bar/line charts. Keep slides spare — the darkness does the drama, so don't
crowd it. Tokens: `assets/style-tokens/futuristic-tech.json`.
