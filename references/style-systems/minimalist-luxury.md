# Minimalist Luxury Branding  (`minimalist-luxury`)

Premium and restrained. Lots of whitespace, a serif heading, a single metallic accent.

- **Use for:** brand proposals, board decks, high-end/executive pitches.
- **Palette:** warm off-white bg, near-black ink, muted gold accent.
- **Feel:** generous margins, no bullet glyphs (whitespace separates ideas), a thin rule, framed cover.
- **Avoid for:** dense data dumps — this style wants few words and confident silence.

Let slides breathe; one idea, lots of air. Tokens: `assets/style-tokens/minimalist-luxury.json`.
