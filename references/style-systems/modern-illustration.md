# Modern Illustration Editorial  (`modern-illustration`)

Bright, bold, friendly-but-professional. Plays well with vector illustrations and strong color blocks.

- **Use for:** marketing, thought leadership, conference talks, launch announcements.
- **Palette:** white bg, deep-navy ink, vivid blue + coral accents.
- **Feel:** clean sans, color block on the cover, section bands, an accent rule under titles.
- **Avoid for:** somber/formal financial or legal content.

Great with `comparison` slides and conceptual diagrams. Tokens: `assets/style-tokens/modern-illustration.json`.
