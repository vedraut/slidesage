# Soft 3D Clay  (`soft-clay-3d`)

**Education default.** Warm, rounded, approachable claymorphism. Lowers the stakes and feels human.

- **Use for:** teaching, onboarding, internal enablement, friendly explainers.
- **Palette:** soft lavender bg, deep-violet ink, violet + warm-orange accents.
- **Feel:** rounded shapes, circle motif on cover, section bands, comfortable spacing.
- **Avoid for:** austere financial/board decks (use `minimalist-luxury` or `futuristic-tech`).

Pairs naturally with `worked-example` and `retrieval` archetypes. Tokens: `assets/style-tokens/soft-clay-3d.json`.
